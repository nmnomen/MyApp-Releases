1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Nikolay Milovidov ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with our applications: CopyBara, BabyBara, Just Currency Converter, and Frugal (collectively, "Apps").

Key Principle: All applications are designed to operate entirely offline and store all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

2. Data Collection and Storage

2.1 What Data is Stored

Each application stores data locally on your device:

  • CopyBara: Text templates, button configurations, and application settings
  • BabyBara: Baby tracking data (feeding, sleeping, pumping, weight records), application settings, and user preferences
  • Just Currency Converter: Currency conversion history, favorite currencies, custom exchange rates, and application settings
  • Frugal: Price comparison data, product entries, calculation history, and application settings

2.2 Where Data is Stored

All application data is stored exclusively in:

  • Android Apps (BabyBara, Just Currency Converter, Frugal): Local device storage in the app's private directory
  • Windows App (CopyBara): Local application data folder on your computer

No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

3. Internet Permissions

Some applications require internet permissions for specific functionality:

  • Just Currency Converter: Requires internet connection to fetch live exchange rates. Exchange rate data is retrieved but not stored with user identification
  • BabyBara: No internet connection required for core functionality
  • CopyBara: No internet connection required
  • Frugal: No internet connection required - works completely offline

4. Third-Party Services

Our applications do not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, security depends on your device's security measures:

  • We recommend using device security features (passcodes, biometrics)
  • Regularly update your device's operating system
  • Use device encryption if available

6. Data Export and Backup

Some applications offer export functionality:

  • BabyBara: Allows export of tracking data to local files for backup purposes. Exporting creates files on your device, and you are responsible for their safekeeping
  • CopyBara: Allows saving templates to files for sharing or backup. Exporting creates files on your device, and you are responsible for their safekeeping
  • Just Currency Converter: Conversion history can be cleared but not exported
  • Frugal: Price comparison data can be cleared, but not exported

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under 13. BabyBara is designed for parents to track their children's data, but does not collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data:

  • You can delete data within each application
  • You can uninstall the application to remove all associated data
  • You can use device backup features to preserve your data
  • You can export your data for personal backup purposes
