Installation & Usage

Installation

1. Download the EXE file
2. Right-click the file and open "Properties"
3. At the bottom of the "General" tab, check "Unblock" and click "OK"
Alternative: In the SmartScreen window, click "More info" -> "Run anyway"

How to Use

• Create buttons with your frequent text
• Click to copy to clipboard
• Use drag and drop to organize
• Color-code for different categories
• Keep window always on top for quick access
