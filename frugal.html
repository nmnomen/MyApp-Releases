Frugal

Smart price comparison tool with multilingual support

Compare prices per unit to find the best deals and save money on your purchases. Whether you're buying groceries, household items, or comparing product sizes, Frugal calculates prices per unit to reveal the true cost and help you make smarter shopping decisions. Fully localized in English, Russian, and Spanish with automatic language detection. All data is stored locally on your device for privacy.

How to Use Frugal

Select Mode

Choose between Weight Mode (for grams/kg or milliliters/liters) or Quantity Mode (for individual pieces)

Enter Price

Input the total price of the product you want to compare

Enter Weight/Quantity

In Weight Mode: enter weight in grams or volume in milliliters
In Quantity Mode: enter the number of pieces

Add Product

Click "Add" to include the product in your comparison list
Repeat for all products you want to compare

Real-World Example

When comparing cereals:

  • Product 1: 500g for $4.99 → $9.98/kg
  • Product 2: 750g for $6.49 → $8.65/kg
  • Product 3: 1kg for $8.99 → $8.99/kg

Frugal automatically calculates these unit prices and highlights Product 2 as the best value, even though it's not the cheapest package overall.
